Meet Alice Precious

10+

Years Experience

Helping stories find their audience.

Alice Precious is an independent book marketing professional with ten years of experience, passionate about helping authors and publishers navigate the world of book promotion and literary visibility.

The philosophy is simple: publishing a book is an important achievement, but helping that book reach its audience is another essential part of its journey. Through personalized marketing strategies and thoughtfully developed promotional campaigns, I support authors and publishers at different stages of their publishing journey.

"My commitment is to approach every project with creativity, professionalism, integrity, and a genuine understanding of the author's goals."

Alice Precious
